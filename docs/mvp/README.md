# Nebula AI — MVP Cut (Solo Founder, 90 Days)

> **Review panel:** CTO + CPO + VC  
> **Constraints:** 1 founder · 12 months runway · first revenue in 90 days · 100 paying users  
> **Rule:** Ship > Learn > Revenue. Elegance and scale are explicitly out of scope.

## Verdict on v2 Platform Architecture

The v2 design is **correct for Year 2**, **fatal for Month 1**.

A solo founder cannot build 10 agents, Temporal, Firecracker, 6 queues, artifact registry, and preview fleet in 90 days. Attempting it guarantees zero revenue and zero learning.

**MVP promise (what we sell):**  
*"Describe your app. Watch it get built. Use it in your browser. Pay to keep building."*

**Not in MVP:** GitHub export, CI/CD, multi-model routing, test generation, enterprise security, templates marketplace.
